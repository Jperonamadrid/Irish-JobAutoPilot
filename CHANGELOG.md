# What's new in JobPilot

JobPilot checks this file to tell you what changed when a new version is out. To release a
version: raise `version` in package.json, add a section here with the same number, push to main.

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-12)
- Uses Claude Opus by default for everything. Choose Opus, Sonnet or Haiku for scoring and for writing in Profile → Fine-tuning.
- Updates: JobPilot tells you when a new version is out and what changed, and installs it with one click (your CV, answers and jobs are kept).
- One-step installers for Windows, macOS and Linux: one command installs everything JobPilot needs.
- More places to find jobs: RecruitIreland.com, Remote OK, and company job pages on SmartRecruiters, Recruitee and Personio.
- Companies you'd love to work for: paste a careers page or a name and their jobs are checked on every run.
- Automatic daily run at a time you choose, with a notification when it finishes.
- Results tab: record replies, interviews and offers, get follow-up reminders, and see what gets you answers.
- Interview prep for jobs you have an interview for, and a ready-to-send follow-up email.
- "Not for me" asks for a quick reason, and scoring learns from your choices.
- Fixes: changes you make while a run is going are no longer lost; one cover letter per job; stronger check that tailored CVs never add facts; better duplicate detection; complete log files.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-12)
- First version: job search across Irish job sites, AI scoring, tailored CVs and cover letters, market report, map, career quiz, and "Help me apply" / "Apply for me" modes.
